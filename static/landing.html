Open the app

Optic disc analysis, from a phone.

A 3D-printed lens turns a smartphone into a fundus camera. Discern calculates cup-to-disc ratios to predict ASD severity through machine learning, with an expert-review interface to correct predictions and improve the model over time.

Motivation

Retinal imaging shouldn't stay inside the hospital.

  1. 01
    Access

    Retinal cameras are large and expensive, so retinal photos are mostly taken in hospitals.

  2. 02
    Research

    My previous research found a correlation between optic cup-to-disc ratios and autism severity, which inspired me to develop Discern to measure these features consistently.

  3. 03
    Reach

    A 3D-printed camera and Discern can make retinal imaging more accessible to underserved communities without expensive retinal imaging equipment.

The hardware

A fundus camera, printed and clipped to a phone.

A lens barrel with an eyecup and focusing rings sits on a 3D-printed sliding clamp that holds the phone camera in line with the optics. It went through several designs to get here.

The pipeline

From a phone photo to a reviewed result.

  1. 01
    Capture

    Photograph the retina through the adapter, then upload a JPG, PNG or DICOM from any browser.

  2. 02
    Segment

    SegFormer-B3 finds the optic disc, crops around it and outlines the disc and cup again at higher zoom.

  3. 03
    Measure & score

    Vertical, horizontal and area cup-to-disc ratios; features of the disc region feed the ASD severity model.

  4. 04
    Review

    An administrator checks the outlines and the severity, corrects them if needed, and releases the result.

What it took

From a printed part to a deployed model.

  1. 01Hardware

    Print the adapter

    Started with a simple phone holder, then a rod-based design and a sliding tube before the final rotating focus ring and adjustable phone clamp.

  2. 02Segmentation

    Find the disc and cup

    SegFormer-B3 first identifies the optic disc in the full retinal image, then analyzes a cropped region at higher resolution to refine the optic disc and cup boundaries.

    • SegFormer-B3
    • REFUGE
    • Two passes
  3. 03Measurement

    Measure the ratios

    Discern automatically calculates vertical, horizontal, and area cup-to-disc ratios, along with optic disc diameter, using the segmented masks.

    • Cup-to-disc ratio
    • Disc size
  4. 04Severity

    Score ASD severity

    ResNet-50 extracts features from the optic disc region, which are passed to an MLP classifier for ASD severity prediction. The classifier is retrained using expert-reviewed labels.

    • ResNet-50
    • MLP
    • Retrains every 20 labels
  5. 05Review

    Close the loop

    Admins confirm or correct the outlines and the severity. Corrections are collected to fine-tune the segmenter.

    • Admin review
    • Outline correction
  6. 06Deployment

    Ship it

    Both models run on CPU with ONNX Runtime in Singapore. Uploads and reviews are stored in Postgres.

    • ONNX Runtime
    • Vercel · Singapore
    • Neon Postgres
