The challenge

The signal sits in a small disc at the back of the eye.

The paper this app reproduces relates the optic nerve head to ASD severity. Using that outside a lab takes two things: a retinal photo that can be taken anywhere, and a disc measurement that does not depend on who is looking.

// Hard

  • Desktop fundus cameras are large and stay in the clinic
  • The optic disc fills only a small part of the photograph
  • Disc and cup boundaries are easy to misjudge by eye
  • A model score on its own is not a result

// Answered

  • A 3D-printed adapter turns a smartphone into the camera
  • The disc is found, cropped and segmented again at higher zoom
  • Cup-to-disc ratios are computed from the masks, not eyeballed
  • Results can be held until an administrator reviews them
The hardware

A fundus camera, printed and clipped to a phone.

A lens barrel with an eyecup and focusing rings sits on a 3D-printed sliding clamp that holds the phone camera in line with the optics. It went through several designs to get here.

The pipeline

From a phone photo to a reviewed result.

  1. 01
    Capture

    Photograph the retina through the adapter, then upload a JPG, PNG or DICOM from any browser.

  2. 02
    Segment

    SegFormer-B3 finds the optic disc, crops around it and outlines the disc and cup again at higher zoom.

  3. 03
    Measure & score

    Vertical, horizontal and area cup-to-disc ratios; features of the disc region feed the ASD severity model.

  4. 04
    Review

    An administrator checks the outlines and the severity, corrects them if needed, and releases the result.

What it took

From a printed part to a deployed model.

  1. 01Hardware

    Print the adapter

    A first holder, then a rod design, then a sliding tube that lines the phone camera up with the lens.

  2. 02Segmentation

    Find the disc and cup

    SegFormer-B3, trained on REFUGE, runs on the whole photo and again on a crop around the disc.

    • SegFormer-B3
    • REFUGE
    • Two passes
  3. 03Measurement

    Measure the ratios

    Vertical, horizontal and area cup-to-disc ratios and disc diameter, computed from the masks.

    • Cup-to-disc ratio
    • Disc size
  4. 04Severity

    Score ASD severity

    ResNet-50 features of the disc region feed a small network trained only on labels an administrator approved.

    • ResNet-50
    • MLP
    • Retrains every 20 labels
  5. 05Review

    Close the loop

    Admins confirm or correct the outlines and the severity. Corrections are collected to fine-tune the segmenter.

    • Admin review
    • Outline correction
  6. 06Deployment

    Ship it

    Both models run on CPU with ONNX Runtime in Singapore. Uploads and reviews are stored in Postgres.

    • ONNX Runtime
    • Vercel · Singapore
    • Neon Postgres
